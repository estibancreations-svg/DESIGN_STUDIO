## Avatar State ownership
Preserve the approved three-board hierarchy:
- **A Cast Board** — active multi-character scene state and fallout.
- **Character Detail Specifications Board** — canonical identity, body, voice, wardrobe, injury/change and provenance anchors.
- **360 View Board** — canonical character generation/camera reference.

Production standard:
- **32 views** for new/canonical or materially changed identity.
- **16 views** for clothing-only or limited non-identity state changes.
- Do not rename the 360 View Board to a number-specific board name.

## Global Place + People Reference Catalog
The catalog is a rights-aware worldwide reference federation for believable places, populations, activity, ambient sound and production context.

Discovery/reference classes include:
- maps and geographic systems;
- travel and tourism sites/apps;
- hotel/lodging and booking systems;
- cruise lines and transport networks;
- event / meetup / community sources;
- landmarks, venue and architectural references;
- social media and public photography;
- cultural, historical, weather and local-activity sources.

Required provenance chain:
`SOURCE → RIGHTS → PROVENANCE → LOCATION → DATE/TIME → ATTRIBUTES → USAGE CLASS → DERIVED KNOWLEDGE`

Public availability or attribution alone is **not** reuse permission. Restricted sources may be used for lookup/display/manual reference when their terms permit, but copying, ingestion, training, redistribution or commercial reuse requires the applicable rights or agreement.

Identifiable people from external imagery should not automatically become copied avatar identities. The system may derive non-identifying population/context attributes and create original avatars consistent with the setting.
