# Standalone leases and creator marketplace

Canonical name remains **VisionWeaver | Design Studio**. A shared codebase serves the VisionWeaver page and standalone customers. Tenant data, membership, quotas, billing and licensing must remain isolated; white-label contracts do not permit a bypass of platform safety.

## Product lines

1. Creator subscription: private catalog, scene drafts, storage and a metered generation allowance after provider qualification.
2. Studio/team lease: team roles, shared assets, version approvals, quota caps, usage reports, audit and support.
3. Enterprise/white-label lease: negotiated branding, isolation, deployment, approved connectors and service commitments.
4. Asset marketplace: licenses for original/re-sublicensable character, wardrobe, performance, environment and effects packages.

No prices, sales forecasts, revenue splits, subscription limits or active leases are asserted. Decide prices from measured storage/render/support/payment costs. Maintain margin and quota enforcement independently of a customer's browser.

## Listing contract

Every listing states exactly what is included: image references versus real 3D mesh/rig, tested motion/voice, formats, software versions, visual variants, render examples, technical limits, creator, provenance, permitted and prohibited uses, update/support terms and refunds. Mark draft previews and unverified claims clearly. Never imply a turnaround image is a game-ready character. Consumers acquire a versioned license entitlement; new versions do not mutate existing productions.

Distinguish personal, commercial finished-media, interactive-app/game, studio/team, commissioned/exclusive, and source redistribution rights. Modification permission is separate from resale. Model training rights require a separate decision. Fab/Mixamo/MetaHuman and other upstream assets need item-specific review; permission for a commercial film is not blanket standalone resale permission. Store rights receipts and evidence, not just a creator checkbox.

## Seller operations

Curated Estiban-owned packages first. External creators require identity/payment onboarding, rights review, content review, technical validation, dispute/takedown handling and a support commitment. Payments require verified webhooks, replay protection, reconciliation, refunds, chargebacks, tax handling and payout controls. Grant entitlements only from verified payment events, never a browser success redirect. Suspensions prevent future restricted actions while preserving required historical records and lawful customer rights.

## Access model

Roles: owner, administrator, editor, reviewer, viewer, seller and platform incident handler. Current database implements owner plus service-managed viewer/editor/admin membership; reviewer/seller/incident workflows are planned. API tokens and OAuth client permissions are distinct from interactive login. A user cannot grant themselves verification, a paid entitlement or a platform role.

## Release evidence

- Credentialed OAuth callback and recovery/logout tests; age/access verification with privacy review.
- Tenant-isolation and direct-API bypass tests, secure secrets, network allowlisting and shared rate limits.
- Trusted media moderation and appeal operations with safety policy versioning.
- Rights, sublicensing, privacy, acceptable use, incident and takedown terms reviewed by counsel.
- Actual billing/payout receipts and entitlement reconciliation tests.
- Accurate formats, interoperability, retention/backup/restore, accessibility and mobile/browser tests.
- Deployment URL, exact tested commit, build/health evidence and rollback plan.

Until these pass, the Store is an honest empty state and the app is a draft-authoring foundation. The existing MIT software license remains in effect. It is not a customer subscription, a paid entitlement, or permission to use third-party likenesses or merchant assets. Exclusive software licensing is unresolved; do not market this MIT code as exclusively proprietary.
