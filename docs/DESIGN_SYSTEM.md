## Avatar and Sims model

Stable identity holds provenance and canonical anchors. Appearance states hold clothing, hair/beard, glasses, marks/injuries, age and physical differences. Voice and performance version independently. Scene knowledge/intention and perceptual access determine reactions. Technical capability labels distinguish image references, rigged/animated characters, interactive performers and tested scene packages. Never claim a photo montage proves calibrated 3D geometry or a usable rig.

Cultural identity, nationality, ethnicity, language, origin, upbringing and residence are independent authored fields. Regional collections need within-group variety, review of claimed historical/cultural authenticity, ordinary contemporary examples and careful handling of ceremonial or culturally significant material. Ethnicity does not determine personality, intelligence, occupation, morality or accent. Representation includes different skin tones, hair textures, body sizes, disabilities, ages, families and professions.

## Scene and world layers

Spaces include geometry, entrances, furniture, transit paths and character scale. Location Necessities include place, era, season, time zone, climate, ecology and local activity. World Anchors include sourced landmarks, skyline, streets and recognizable buildings appropriate to date. Cast references approved states. Objects include props, vehicles, equipment and product-linked wardrobe. Atmosphere, lighting, sound and cameras remain independent layers with causal dependencies.

Enclosed rooms require walls, doors, occlusion, contact, reflections and acoustic treatment. Open spaces require distance, weather, wind and activity plausibility. Vehicles/starships need persistent exterior geometry, compartment maps, access routes, stations, controls, machinery effects, occupants and damage. Switching location cannot silently reset injuries, remove glasses, change body size or lose a held object.

Loading modes: fast editing mode assembles assets immediately; optional presentation mode animates a world-forming transition. Neither mode invents missing licensed assets. Report each loaded asset and unresolved dependency. Matrix references inspire modular staging and performance packages, not copying protected characters, scripts or sets.

## Performance and time

Packages describe compatible rigs/body plans, gestures, gait, speech, expressions, contact markers, props and space. Test different bodies and proportions rather than transferring motion blindly. A labeled ability is not a demonstrated capability.

Time Stretch changes a shot's presentation speed. Orbit Freeze moves a camera around an almost still moment. Selective Time applies separate authored rates to participants. Define event time, editorial time, camera time, per-subject rate, sound treatment, perception, collisions, activation/termination and recovery consequences. Editorial slow motion preserves story causation; a character acting while others slow changes the authored story. Start validation with a falling glass and contact sequence before complex action.

Stimulus → perception → reaction must respect sound arrival, visibility, attention and physical contact. Impacts, explosions, motion, particles, wardrobe movement, dialogue, room tone and score need coordinated but separate controls. Muting sound does not erase a causal event. Removing an event marks dependent effects and reactions for review.

## Store and licensing

Products may include characters, wardrobe expansions, environments, performance packs and effects. Each listing shows neutral and showcase previews, exact files, capability level, tested tools, known limits, source rights, modification scope and support. License versions pin buyer entitlements. Updates are opt-in for existing productions. Product-placement merchant links and asset marketplace licenses are independent. Direct merchant links do not prove exact visual match, stock or affiliate rights.

## Proposed reusable technology

OpenUSD for scene composition/variants; Blender for modeling/rigging/controlled production; Three.js or Babylon.js for a future interactive 3D viewport; Adobe Substance 3D for texturing; selected Mixamo workflows for compatible humanoids; MetaHuman subject to precise licensing; OpenTimelineIO for editorial interchange. Existing repositories treat these as candidates, not installed or certified adapters. GitHub popularity or a commercial-use statement is not a security review or resale authorization.

Tool registry fields: version, license, allowed redistribution, dependency inventory, maintenance status, security review, purpose, supported inputs/outputs, integration tests, hardware requirements, costs, performance measurements, known failures, verified date and actual receipt IDs. New equipment purchase is not required by the present draft editor. Capture/rig/render hardware requirements remain dependent on tested production methods.

## Output and autonomy

Outputs: review sheets, images/video when generated, editable versioned project metadata, actual 3D/animation files when present and permitted, and licensed packages with dependency/rights manifests. No “game ready” label without a tested package. Approve look/feel and a bounded policy once; routine jobs may run within approved limits, with budget/retry caps and an exception queue. New likeness, changed canon, unknown rights, unsupported physics or repeated QC failure must stop for review. Every provider handoff logs actual references and receipts; unsupported inputs cannot silently disappear.
